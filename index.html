    About Cykes

    Cykes is a free, simple map of hiking trails in Cyprus. No account, no ads.

    Trail data comes from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License. Trails are refreshed from OpenStreetMap every week. If a trail is missing or wrong, you can fix it on OpenStreetMap and it will show up here.

    Elevation is estimated from NASA SRTM 30 m data via Open Topo Data. Difficulty comes from OpenStreetMap where mappers set it, otherwise it is estimated from length and climbing and marked "estimated".

    Maps: OpenStreetMap standard tiles, and OpenTopoMap (CC-BY-SA).

    Always check conditions before you go. Trails can be closed after fires or storms, and summer heat in Cyprus is serious: start early and carry plenty of water.
